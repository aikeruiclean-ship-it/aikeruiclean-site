## Hidden Costs First-Time Buyers Miss

- **Shipping/freight**: $500-5,000 (domestic vs international)
- **Import duties**: 0-35% depending on country
- **Operator training**: 1-4 hours of labor
- **Spare parts inventory**: $200-500 initial stock
- **Charging station**: $200-1,000 electrical work
- **Battery watering system**: $50-150 (lead-acid only)

---

## The Bottom Line

A quality walk-behind scrubber costs $3,000-12,000 upfront and $1,500-4,000/year to operate. A ride-on costs $9,000-35,000 upfront and $2,500-6,000/year. But factory-direct buyers save 30-50% on both — without sacrificing quality.
