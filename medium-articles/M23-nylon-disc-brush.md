Why a Nylon Brush Plate

The nylon brush plate is soft to the touch, which means it is gentle on your floor finish while still lifting daily soil. Its bristles distribute liquid evenly across the surface, so the cleaning solution works where it should and dries evenly.

Benefits of nylon for daily floor cleaning:

- Soft on floors - no scratching on tile, concrete, or epoxy
- Even liquid output - cleaning solution is applied uniformly
- Low maintenance - easy to rinse and quick to dry
- Consistent results - the same clean from corner to corner
