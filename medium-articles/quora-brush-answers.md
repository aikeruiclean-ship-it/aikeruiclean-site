Q1: What type of brush is best for my floor scrubber?
URL: https://www.quora.com/What-type-of-brush-is-best-for-my-floor-scrubber
Answer:
The right brush depends on your floor type. For smooth concrete, use a medium nylon disc brush. For tile or epoxy, use a soft nylon brush to avoid scratching. For heavy grease or industrial grime, switch to a polypropylene (PPL) brush. For stripping old floor finish, use an abrasive brush. Q2: When should I replace my floor scrubber brush?
URL: https://www.quora.com/When-should-I-replace-my-floor-scrubber-brush
Answer:
Replace your disc brush when bristles are worn to less than 1/2 inch. Replace roller brushes at 1/4 inch. Signs you need a new brush: the machine leaves streaks, takes longer to clean the same area, or the bristles are visibly bent or frayed. Disc brushes typically last 6-12 months with daily use. Q3: Are all floor scrubber brushes interchangeable between brands?
URL: https://www.quora.com/Are-all-floor-scrubber-brushes-interchangeable-between-brands
Answer:
No. The connection type varies by brand. Most machines use a 2-lug NP-9200 clutch plate (Tennant, Viper, Nilfisk, Comac, most Chinese OEM). Some use a 3-lug center lock (Advance, older Nilfisk). Others use a 4-lug magnetic system (Karcher, Hako). Count the lugs on your current brush and measure the center hole before ordering. We offer brushes for all three types: aikeruiclean.com/parts

Q4: What is the difference between a disc brush and a roller brush for floor scrubbers?
URL: https://www.quora.com/What-is-the-difference-between-a-disc-brush-and-a-roller-brush-for-floor-scrubbers
Answer:
Disc brushes spin in one direction and are best for smooth, flat floors like concrete and tile. They are the most common type. Roller brushes (cylindrical) use two counter-rotating tubes and are better for uneven or textured floors. Roller brushes can also pick up larger debris, reducing the need to sweep first. Disc brushes cost less and are easier to replace. Compare both types: aikeruiclean.com/guides/disc-brush-vs-roller-brush-scrubber
