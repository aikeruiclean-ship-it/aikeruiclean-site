## Step 1: Find a Real Manufacturer (Not a Trading Company)

This is the #1 mistake. Many "manufacturers" on Alibaba are actually trading companies — middlemen who add 20–40% markup and have zero control over quality.

**How to verify:**

- **Ask for a live video tour** — A real factory will happily show you the production floor. A trading company can't.
- **Check the factory address on Google Maps** — Real factories are in industrial zones. Trading companies operate from office buildings.
- **Request certification copies** — CE and ISO 9001:2015 are the baseline. SGS or Bureau Veritas inspection reports are better.
- **Look at their website** — A real manufacturer has detailed product specs, not just stock photos and generic descriptions.

---

## Step 2: Understand the Pricing

Chinese floor scrubber pricing follows a simple rule: **the shorter the supply chain, the lower the price.**

| Supply Chain | Typical Markup | Example Price (Walk-Behind) |
|-------------|---------------|---------------------------|
| Factory → You (Direct) | 0% | $3,000–$6,000 |
| Factory → Dealer → You | 20–40% | $4,000–$9,000 |
| Factory → Importer → Distributor → Dealer → You | 50–80% | $6,000–$12,000 |

**Pricing breakdown for a typical walk-behind scrubber:**
- Machine cost (FOB): $3,000–$6,000
- Shipping (sea freight): $300–$800
- Customs & duties: 2–8% of declared value (varies by country)
- Total: $3,500–$7,500 delivered

A comparable Tennant or Nilfisk machine from a local dealer: $8,000–$15,000.

## Step 3: Choose the Right Machine for Your Facility

Walk-behind vs ride-on depends on facility size:

| Facility Size | Recommended Type | Price Range (Direct) |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Under 10,000 sq ft | Mop or small walk-behind (17–20 inch) | $1,800–$4,000 |
| 10,000–30,000 sq ft | Walk-behind (20–28 inch) | $3,000–$7,500 |
| 30,000–80,000 sq ft | Ride-on (28–36 inch) | $6,000–$15,000 |
| 80,000+ sq ft | Large ride-on (36–50 inch) | $12,000–$24,000 |

**Battery selection:**
- **Lead-acid** ($400–$800): Lower upfront cost but needs daily maintenance. 1–2 year lifespan.
- **Lithium** ($1,200–$2,500): Higher upfront, zero maintenance, 3–5 year lifespan. Supports opportunity charging (top up during breaks).

For most facilities, lithium pays for itself in labor savings within 18 months.

## Step 4: Shipping & Logistics

**Sea freight** is the standard method for floor scrubbers:

| Route | Transit Time | Cost (20ft container) |
|-------|------------|----------------------|
| China → US West Coast | 15–20 days | $1,500–$2,500 |
| China → US East Coast | 25–35 days | $2,500–$3,500 |
| China → Europe | 20–30 days | $2,000–$3,000 |
| China → Middle East | 15–25 days | $1,500–$2,500 |

**Documents required:**
- Commercial invoice
- Packing list
- Bill of lading
- Certificate of origin (for preferential tariff rates)
- CE declaration of conformity

---

## Step 5: Customs Clearance

Floor scrubbers typically fall under **HS Code 8479.89** (Machines having individual functions). Import duties range from 0% (some trade agreements) to 8%.

**Pro tip:** Ask your supplier to split the shipment into machine + parts if possible. Parts often have lower duty rates than complete machines.

---

## Step 6: After-Sales Support

The most common concern importers have: "What happens when the machine breaks?"

Here's what a reliable manufacturer should provide:
- **1-year warranty** on all machine components
- **Spare parts in stock** — verify they stock common wear items (brushes, squeegees, filters)
- **Video-based technical support** — live or recorded troubleshooting guides
- **English documentation** — user manual, parts diagram, wiring diagram
