# How to Import Floor Scrubbers from China — Complete 2026 Guide

**By Mark Xu | Sales Director at Aikerui Cleaning Technology**

Importing industrial floor scrubbers from China can save your business 30–50% compared to buying from local dealers. But the process comes with risks: fake factories, hidden fees, incompatible machines, and warranty nightmares.

I've spent 15+ years in the floor cleaning industry and helped importers in 50+ countries source equipment factory-direct. Here's exactly how to do it right — from finding a real manufacturer to taking delivery at your warehouse door.

## Step 1: Find a Real Manufacturer (Not a Trading Company)

This is the #1 mistake importers make. Many "manufacturers" on Alibaba and Made-in-China are actually **trading companies** — middlemen who add 20–40% markup and have zero control over quality or lead times.

**How to tell the difference:**

| Verification Method | Real Manufacturer | Trading Company |
|-------------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Factory video tour | Gladly arranges a live walk-through | Makes excuses ("manager is busy") |
| Factory address | Located in industrial zone | CBD office building |
| Certifications | CE, ISO 9001, SGS available on request | "We can get them" |
| Product specs | Detailed technical data sheets | Generic descriptions, stock photos |
| Spare parts inventory | Stocks 300+ SKUs in-house | Orders from factories after you order |

**My recommended verification process:**

1. **Step 1 — Google Maps the address.** Paste their claimed factory address into Google Maps. If it shows an office building instead of a factory compound, that's a red flag.

2. **Step 2 — Request a live video call.** Ask to see the production line, not just the showroom. A legitimate manufacturer will happily do this. We do this daily at Aikerui.

3. **Step 3 — Ask for photos of their QC process.** Real factories have quality control stations. Trading companies don't.

4. **Step 4 — Check their export history.** Ask for past Bill of Lading copies or customs declarations. A manufacturer with 50+ export destinations is a safe bet.

## Step 2: Understand the Real Pricing

Chinese floor scrubber pricing follows a simple rule: **the shorter the supply chain, the lower the price.**

| Supply Chain Model | Typical Markup | Example Price (Walk-Behind 20") |
|------------------|---------------|-------------------------------|
| Factory → You (Direct) | **0%** | **$3,000–$6,000** ✅ |
| Factory → Dealer → You | 20–40% | $4,000–$9,000 |
| Factory → Importer → Distributor → Dealer → You | 50–80% | $6,000–$12,000 |

**Complete cost breakdown for a walk-behind scrubber (factory-direct):**

```
Machine cost (FOB Shanghai):     $3,000–$6,000
Ocean freight (20ft container):  $300–$800 (shared across multiple units)
Customs clearance & duties:      2–8% of declared value
Inland delivery to your door:    $200–$600
Insurance (0.3–0.5% of value):   $30–$50
────────────────────────────────────────────
Total delivered cost:             $3,500–$7,500
```

**Compare that to a dealer price for a comparable brand:** $8,000–$15,000.

That's a **40–50% savings** — which is why import volume from China has grown 300% in this category over the last 5 years.

## Step 3: Payment Terms — How to Not Get Scammed

This is where most deals fall apart. Here are the standard payment structures:

### Most Common: 30% Deposit + 70% Before Shipment
- **30% upfront** to start production
- **70% balance** after you receive production photos and video
- **Protection:** Ask for photos of the crated machine with shipping marks visible before paying the balance

### Safer Alternative: Letter of Credit (L/C)
- Your bank guarantees payment after documents are presented
- Costs $300–$500 in bank fees
- Best for orders over $20,000

### Trade Assurance (Alibaba)
- Alibaba holds funds in escrow
- Released to supplier after you confirm receipt
- Max coverage typically $50,000
- Adds 2–3% in fees

**Red flag:** Any supplier asking for 100% payment upfront without exceptions. No legitimate manufacturer requires this.

## Step 4: Choose the Right Machine for Your Facility

Walk-behind vs ride-on depends on facility size and cleaning frequency:

| Facility Size | Best Machine Type | Cleaning Speed | Factory-Direct Price |
|--------------|------------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Under 10,000 sq ft | Small walk-behind (17–20 inch) | 12,000–18,000 sq ft/hr | $1,800–$4,000 |
| 10,000–30,000 sq ft | Walk-behind (20–28 inch) | 18,000–35,000 sq ft/hr | $3,000–$7,500 |
| 30,000–80,000 sq ft | Ride-on (28–36 inch) | 35,000–70,000 sq ft/hr | $6,000–$15,000 |
| 80,000+ sq ft | Large ride-on (36–50 inch) | 70,000+ sq ft/hr | $12,000–$24,000 |

### Battery Decision: Lead-Acid vs Lithium

| Factor | Lead-Acid | Lithium |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Upfront cost | **$400–$800** ✅ | $1,200–$2,500 |
| Lifespan | 1–2 years | **3–5 years** ✅ |
| Daily maintenance | Watering terminals, cleaning | **None** ✅ |
| Opportunity charging | Not recommended | **Fully supported** ✅ |
| 5-year total cost | $2,800–$4,000 | **$1,800–$3,000** ✅ |

**Bottom line:** Lithium batteries cost more upfront but save money within 18 months through longer life and zero maintenance.

## Step 5: Pre-Shipment Inspection

Never skip this step. Here's my recommended inspection process:

1. **Production photos** — Request photos of the finished machine before crating
2. **Third-party inspection** — Hire SGS or Bureau Veritas ($300–$500 per inspection)
3. **Test run video** — Ask for a video of the machine running with water in both tanks
4. **Crating photos** — Confirm ISPM 15 certified wooden crate with proper markings
5. **Loading photos** — Container loaded with machine secured inside

At Aikerui, we send all five stages of photos and videos to every buyer as standard practice.

## Step 6: Shipping & Logistics

Sea freight is the most cost-effective method. Here are typical routes and costs:

| Route | Transit Time | Sea Freight (shared) | Air Freight (urgent) |
|-------|:----------:|:------------------:|:------------------:|
| China → US West Coast | 15–20 days | $300–$600 | $1,500–$3,000 |
| China → US East Coast | 25–35 days | $500–$800 | $2,000–$3,500 |
| China → Europe (Rotterdam) | 20–30 days | $400–$700 | $1,800–$3,000 |
| China → Middle East (Dubai) | 15–25 days | $300–$500 | $1,500–$2,500 |
| China → Australia | 15–20 days | $300–$500 | $1,500–$2,500 |

**Documents you'll need for customs clearance:**

- Commercial invoice (from supplier)
- Packing list
- Bill of lading (from freight forwarder)
- Certificate of origin (for preferential tariff rates under free trade agreements)
- CE declaration of conformity
- Marine cargo insurance certificate

**Pro tip:** Air freight is 5–8x more expensive than sea freight. Only use it for urgent spare parts orders, not full machines.

## Step 7: Customs Clearance

Floor scrubbers typically fall under **HS Code 8479.89** — "Machines having individual functions, not specified elsewhere."

| Import Country | Duty Rate | Notes |
|---------------|:---------:|-------|
| United States | 0–3.7% | Section 301 tariffs may apply; check current status |
| European Union | 0–2.7% | 0% if Certificate of Origin provided |
| Australia | 0% | Under China-Australia Free Trade Agreement |
| Middle East (UAE) | 0–5% | Varies by emirate |
| Southeast Asia | 0–5% | Lower rates under ASEAN-China FTA |

**Pro tip:** Ask your supplier to split the shipment into machine + spare parts on separate line items. Spare parts often have lower duty rates than complete machines.

## Step 8: After-Sales Support Plan

"Help — my machine stopped working and the supplier isn't responding." This is the most common complaint I hear from importers who chose the wrong supplier.

Here's what a reliable manufacturer should provide — verify **before** you order:

- **1-year warranty** on all machine components
- **Spare parts stock** — confirm they stock brushes, squeegees, filters, and hoses for your specific model
- **Video-based technical support** — willing to do live video calls for troubleshooting
- **English documentation** — user manual, parts diagram, wiring diagram, all in English
- **Response time commitment** — they should reply within 24 hours (time zone difference is not an excuse)
