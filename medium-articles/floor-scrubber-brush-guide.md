# Floor Scrubber Brush: Types, Materials & How to Choose the Right One

**By Mark Xu | Sales Director at Aikerui Cleaning Technology**

The brush is the part of your floor scrubber that actually does the cleaning. Pick the wrong one, and you'll damage your floor, leave dirt behind, or burn through brushes every few months.

Here's everything you need to know about floor scrubber brushes — types, materials, compatibility, and when to replace.

---

## The 3 Types of Floor Scrubber Brushes

### 1. Disc Brushes (Rotary)
The most common type. A circular brush spins in one direction to scrub the floor. Best for smooth, flat surfaces like concrete, tile, and epoxy. Available in 13–50 inch diameters. Disc brushes are the most affordable and widely available.

### 2. Cylindrical Brushes (Roller)
Two counter-rotating tube-shaped brushes that lift debris as they scrub. Better for uneven or textured floors. Common on sweeper-scrubber combo machines. They can pick up larger debris, reducing the need to sweep beforehand.

### 3. Square Oscillating Pads
A flat pad vibrates at high speed. Best for cleaning tight against walls and corners. Less common but growing in popularity.

## Brush Material — Which One Do You Need?

| Material | Stiffness | Best For | Avoid On |
|----------|:---------:|----------|----------|
| Nylon | Medium | Tile, concrete, epoxy — daily cleaning | — |
| Polypropylene (PPL) | Stiff | Heavy grease, industrial grime | Soft floors, epoxy |
| Abrasive (SC/Tynex) | Very stiff | Stripping finish, deep cleaning | Daily use, sealed floors |
| Steel Wire | Extreme | Heavy debris on industrial concrete | Any finished floor |
| Natural Fiber | Soft | Polishing wood, marble, linoleum | Heavy soil |

---

## Quick Selection Guide

| Floor Type | Recommended Brush |
|------------|-----------------|
| Smooth concrete | Nylon disc brush (medium) |
| Epoxy / sealed concrete | Nylon disc brush (soft-medium) |
| Tile / ceramic | Nylon disc brush (soft) |
| Wood / marble | Natural fiber or soft nylon |
| Textured concrete | Cylindrical nylon or PPL |
| Heavy grease/oil | PPL disc brush |

## Which Brands Are Compatible?

The connection between the brush and your machine depends on the clutch plate type:

- **NP-9200 (2-Lug):** Fits Tennant T5/T7/T300/T500, Viper, Nilfisk, Comac, most Chinese OEM machines
- **3-Lug Center Lock:** Fits Advance, older Nilfisk, older Viper
- **4-Lug / Magnetic:** Fits Karcher BD50/BD53/B90, Hako B45/B70/B75R

## When to Replace Your Brush

Replace your scrubber brush when:

- Bristles are worn to 50% of original length
- Bristles are bent, frayed, or uneven
- The brush leaves streaks or misses spots
- Disc brush bristles < 1/2 inch → replace
- Roller brush bristles < 1/4 inch → replace

**General rule:** Disc brushes last 6–12 months with daily use. Cylindrical brushes last 8–14 months.
