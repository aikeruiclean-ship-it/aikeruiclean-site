## Why the Brush Head Is the Most Important Part of Your Scrubber

The brush head does the actual cleaning. It scrubs dirt off the floor so the squeegee can pick up the water. A worn or wrong brush head means:

Streaks left behind after cleaning. The squeegee cannot fix what the brush missed.

Dirt pushed around instead of removed. Flattened bristles lose their scrubbing power.

Floor damage. A worn brush with exposed backing plate can scratch concrete or epoxy.

More chemical usage. Operators compensate for a bad brush by using more cleaning solution.

The brush head is also the cheapest part to replace and the easiest to upgrade. Getting it right has an outsized impact on cleaning quality.

## How a Brush Head Is Made (Factory Tour)

The factory video shows the real production line. Here is what happens step by step:

1. Raw material. Virgin nylon or PPL pellets are the starting point. Quality brushes never use recycled fillers.

2. Tufting. Bristles are punched into the backing plate in a precise pattern. Bristle density determines scrubbing power.

3. Trimming. Bristles are cut to a uniform height, usually 20 to 25 mm. Rounded bristle tips protect the floor.

4. Inspection. Every brush is checked for bristle density, lug alignment, and backing integrity before shipment.

5. Packing. Brushes are export-packed and shipped within 24 hours for in-stock items.

## How to Know a Brush Head Is Quality

Not all brushes are equal, even when they look the same. Check these:

Virgin materials. Ask if the bristles are virgin nylon or PPL. Recycled fillers break down faster.

Rounded bristle tips. Square-cut tips can scratch sealed floors.

Bristle height. At least 20 mm when new. Below 12 mm means it is time to replace.

Bristle density. Dense, even tufting scrubs better and lasts longer.

Lug pattern. Match your machine. Two lugs is the NP-9200 standard, used by Tennant, Viper, Nilfisk, and most Chinese OEM machines.

## How to Order the Right Brush Head

Three things to check before ordering:

1. Count the lugs on your current brush. Two lugs means NP-9200 standard.

2. Measure the diameter. 17, 20, and 24 inch cover 90 percent of walk-behind scrubbers.

3. Note the material. Nylon for daily, PPL for heavy soil, abrasive for stripping.
