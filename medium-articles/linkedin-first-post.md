## 帖子正文（直接复制）

I spent the last 15 years helping facilities buy floor scrubbers. Here is the one mistake I see almost every first-time buyer make.

They buy a machine. Then they buy replacement brushes from the dealer. Every time.

A 20 inch disc brush costs 100 to 250 USD at a dealer. The same brush, from the factory that makes it, costs 45 to 75 USD. Same nylon. Same bristle density. Same lug pattern. The only difference is the label.

Most people do not realize that the biggest cleaning equipment brands do not make their own brushes. They source them from factories in China and mark them up 2 to 3 times.

I run one of those factories. Aikerui has manufactured floor scrubbers, sweepers, and 360 plus replacement parts since 2008. We ship direct to 50 plus countries, with CE and ISO 9001 certification.

If you manage a warehouse, a facility, or a cleaning company, you do not need to overpay for consumables. You just need to know where they come from.

Three things to check before you buy replacement brushes:

1. Count the lugs on your current brush. Two lugs means NP-9200 standard. That fits most Tennant, Nilfisk, Viper, and Chinese OEM machines.

2. Measure the diameter. 17, 20, and 24 inch cover 90 percent of walk-behind scrubbers.

3. Ask where the brush is made. If the answer is "China," you can buy the same product direct from the source.

We publish full pricing and compatibility guides at aikeruiclean.com/parts. Factory tours and third-party inspections are welcome. Anyone can verify.

Happy to answer questions about brush materials, compatibility, or factory-direct sourcing in the comments.
