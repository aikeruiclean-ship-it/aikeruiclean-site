# Floor Scrubber Squeegee Blade Guide: Types, Materials & When to Replace

**By Mark Xu | Sales Director at Aikerui Cleaning Technology**

If your floor scrubber leaves streaks or water behind, the squeegee blades are almost always the culprit. This simple rubber strip is the last line of defense between your machine and a clean, dry floor.

Here's everything you need to know about floor scrubber squeegee blades — types, materials, compatibility, and replacement timing.

---

## What Is a Squeegee Blade?

The squeegee blade is the rubber strip at the back of your floor scrubber. After the brush scrubs and the vacuum lifts the dirty water, the squeegee **wipes the remaining moisture off the floor** so it dries clean.

**Why it matters:** A worn or wrong squeegee blade = streaky, wet floors = safety hazard + wasted labor re-cleaning.

---

## The 2 Main Materials

### Natural Rubber (NR)
| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Flexibility | High — conforms to floor surface |
| Best for | Smooth floors (concrete, tile, epoxy) |
| Lifespan | 3-6 months |
| Cost | $10-25 (factory-direct) |
| Warning | Softer, wears faster on rough surfaces |

### Polyurethane (PU)
| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Flexibility | Medium — stiffer, more durable |
| Best for | Rough/uneven floors, high-traffic areas |
| Lifespan | 6-12 months |
| Cost | $15-35 (factory-direct) |
| Warning | Can chatter on very smooth floors |

**Quick rule:** NR for smooth floors (better wipe), PU for rough floors (better wear).

## Blade Types by Shape

| Type | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Straight blade** | Standard for most machines |
| **Curved blade** | Follows machine curvature for better contact |
| **Double blade** | Two strips — better water pickup on wide machines |
| **Screw-on / Clip-on** | Attachment method varies by brand |

## Signs Your Squeegee Blade Needs Replacing

1. **Streaks left on the floor** — worn edge can't wipe clean
2. **Water puddles behind the machine** — blade not making full contact
3. **Visible wear** — rounded or nicked edges
4. **Machine "chatters"** — blade vibrating instead of gliding
5. **Hardened or cracked rubber** — age or chemical damage

---

## How to Extend Squeegee Blade Life

| Tip | Benefit |
|-----|---------|
| **Rotate blades** (if reversible) | Even wear, 2x lifespan |
| **Clean blades daily** | Remove grit that abrades rubber |
| **Store machine with squeegee up** | Avoids flat-spotting the rubber |
| **Use correct blade material** | NR on smooth, PU on rough |
| **Check alignment monthly** | Proper angle = even contact |
