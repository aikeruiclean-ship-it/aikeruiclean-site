# How to Buy Floor Scrubber Parts Direct From a Chinese Factory (2026 Guide)

Buying floor scrubber parts from China can cut your costs by 30 to 50 percent. But the process only works if you find a real manufacturer, verify the quality, and handle the logistics correctly. This guide walks through the practical steps, based on how a brush factory actually operates.

## Why Buy Direct From the Factory

The brands that sell floor scrubber brushes in your country usually do not make them. They source from factories in China, add their branding, and mark the price up two to three times. When you buy factory-direct, you pay for the product itself, not the brand overhead.

The savings are real. A disc brush that costs 100 to 250 dollars from a dealer is 45 to 75 dollars at the factory. A roller brush that costs 120 to 300 dollars from a dealer is 55 to 95 dollars factory-direct. The brush is the same specification, made on the same type of production line.

## Step 1. Confirm the Supplier Is a Factory, Not a Trader

The biggest risk when sourcing from China is dealing with a trading company that resells products from other factories. A real manufacturer will usually agree to:

A live video tour of the production floor. Ask to see the extrusion lines, bristle trimming, and finished brush assembly on a video call.

A business license that matches an industrial address. Cross-check the address on maps and look for an actual factory site.

Photos of raw material stock and molds. A factory holds nylon, PPL, and abrasive material in volume, plus the tooling for each brush size.

Customer references and export records. Legitimate manufacturers can point to repeat buyers and shipping history.

If a supplier hesitates on any of these, treat it as a warning sign.

## Step 2. Check Compatibility Before You Order

A brush fits a machine by three things: diameter, mounting system, and bristle specification. Most machines use the NP-9200 2-lug standard, which covers Tennant, Nilfisk, Viper, Comac, and most Chinese OEM scrubbers. Some machines need a 3-lug center lock or a 4-lug magnetic mount.

Send the factory your machine model, OEM part number, and a photo of the current part. A good factory confirms compatibility before you place an order, not after.

## Step 3. Order Samples First

Never commit to a bulk order before testing a sample. A factory with stock ships samples within 24 to 48 hours by courier. Compare the sample with your current part on three points:

Bristle density. Count the tufts per square inch. Dense, even packing is the sign of a quality brush.

Bristle height and stiffness. Measure against your original. The sample should match within a millimeter.

Backing plate quality. The plate that connects the brush to the motor must be solid and true, or the brush will wobble.

Most factories deduct the sample cost from your first bulk order.

## Step 4. Compare Quotes on More Than Price

When you compare factory quotes, look beyond the unit price. Ask about:

Minimum order quantity. Standard brushes usually have a low MOQ, sometimes one piece for samples. Custom and branded brushes start around 50 to 200 pieces.

Lead time. Stock items ship in days. Custom production takes two to four weeks.

Payment terms. A common arrangement is 30 percent deposit and 70 percent before shipment, with PayPal or Trade Assurance for smaller first orders.

Beware of quotes that look too good. Suspiciously cheap brushes are often made with recycled filler instead of virgin nylon or PPL, and they shed bristles or wear out fast.

## Step 5. Handle Payment and Shipping Safely

For your first order, protect yourself:

Use Trade Assurance or PayPal for smaller orders.

Consider an inspection service such as SGS or BV before shipment for larger orders.

For shipping, express courier works for parts: 3 to 7 days to the US or Europe. Sea freight is for bulk, taking 25 to 40 days, and makes sense when you order in volume.

The factory should provide a commercial invoice and the correct HS code for customs. Parts usually fall under the machinery parts classification, but confirm the exact code for your destination country.

## Step 6. Build a Long-Term Relationship

The best pricing and priority production come from repeat orders. Keep your drawings, part numbers, and order history with the same factory. Ask about annual volume discounts and stocking programs for the parts you consume fastest.

## A Note on Factory Visits

If you travel to China, a factory visit is the strongest verification. Most real manufacturers welcome visits and will show you the full production process. If you cannot travel, a live video tour is the next best option. Either way, ask to see the production line that makes your specific product, not just the showroom.
