## Why Hair Tangles on Main Brushes

Main brushes on floor washers spin at high speed. Hair, pet fur, and loose fibers wrap around the bristles and gradually build up into tangles. Once tangled, the brush:

- Loses cleaning power — the bristles cannot reach the floor.
- Leaves streaks and stains — dirt is pushed, not removed.
- Wears unevenly — tangled clumps pull the bristles out of shape.
- Forces you to stop and clean the brush every few minutes.

The fix is a brush designed with anti-tangle bristles and the correct density.

## What Makes an Anti-Tangle Main Brush

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|
| High-density bristles | Dense tufting prevents hair from reaching the brush core and wrapping tightly. |
| Moderate hardness | Firm enough to remove deep stains, soft enough to release hair instead of grabbing it. |
| Wear-resistant material | Bristles keep their shape longer, so anti-tangle performance lasts. |
| Rounded bristle tips | Reduce friction so hair slides off instead of catching. |

A brush with all four features resists tangling and keeps cleaning at full power for its whole life.

## Easy Replacement

Replacing a tangled main brush takes minutes:

1. Turn off the machine and remove the key.
2. Lift the brush deck.
3. Slide out the old brush.
4. Slide in the new anti-tangle brush.
5. Secure the end caps and lower the deck.

No special tools, no technician needed. A fresh brush restores full cleaning power instantly.
