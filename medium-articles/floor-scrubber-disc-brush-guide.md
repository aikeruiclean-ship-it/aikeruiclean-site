# Floor Scrubber Disc Brush: Complete Buying Guide — Nylon, PPL & Abrasive

**By Mark Xu | Sales Director at Aikerui Cleaning Technology**

The disc brush is the round brush that spins on your floor scrubber's brush deck — the part that actually scrubs the dirt off your floor. Pick the wrong one and you'll either scratch your floor or leave it dirty.

Here's everything you need to know to choose the right floor scrubber disc brush.

---

## What Is a Floor Scrubber Disc Brush?

A disc brush (also called a rotary brush) mounts to the clutch plate or pad driver under your scrubber and spins in one direction to agitate cleaning solution against the floor.

**Key specs:**
- **Sizes:** 13–50 inch diameters to match your brush deck
- **Connection:** NP-9200 (2-lug), 3-lug center lock, or 4-lug magnetic
- **Materials:** Nylon, polypropylene (PPL), abrasive, steel wire
- **Best for:** Smooth floors — concrete, tile, epoxy, sealed surfaces

---

## Disc Brush Materials — Which One Do You Need?

| Material | Stiffness | Best For | Avoid On |
|----------|:---------:|----------|----------|
| **Nylon** | Medium | Daily cleaning — tile, concrete, epoxy | — |
| **PPL** | Stiff | Heavy grease, oil, industrial grime | Soft floors, epoxy |
| **Abrasive** | Very stiff | Stripping finish, deep cleaning | Daily use |
| **Steel wire** | Extreme | Heavy debris | Any finished floor |
| **Natural fiber** | Soft | Polishing wood, marble, linoleum | Heavy soil |

**Nylon is the standard choice** for most facilities — flexible, durable, and safe on common floor types. Switch to PPL if you have heavy grease buildup. Use abrasive only for occasional deep cleaning or floor stripping.

## Brand Compatibility

Disc brushes connect via a clutch plate. The lug pattern varies by brand:

- **NP-9200 (2-Lug):** Tennant T5/T7/T300/T500, Viper AS510B/AS5160/LS160, Nilfisk SC500/SC600, Comac, IPC, most Chinese OEM
- **3-Lug Center Lock:** Advance, older Nilfisk, older Viper
- **4-Lug / Magnetic:** Karcher BD50/BD53/B90, Hako B45/B70/B75R, some Gaomei

Not sure? Count the lugs on your current brush and measure the center hole. Or send us a photo — we'll confirm within 24 hours.

## When to Replace Your Disc Brush

Replace your disc brush when:

- Bristles worn to less than **1/2 inch (12mm)**
- Bristles bent, frayed, or uneven
- The machine leaves streaks or misses spots
- Typical lifespan: **6–12 months** with daily use
