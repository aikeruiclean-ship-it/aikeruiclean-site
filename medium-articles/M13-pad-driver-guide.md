Floor Scrubber Pad Driver Guide: Types, Compatibility and How to Choose

By Mark Xu, Sales Director at Aikerui Cleaning Technology

The pad driver, also called a pad holder or brush driver, is the disc that connects your floor scrubber or floor machine to its pad or brush. It is the part that transfers the machine's spinning power to the cleaning pad underneath.

Here is what you need to know about floor scrubber pad drivers, including types, compatibility, and how to choose the right one.

---

What Is a Pad Driver?

The pad driver is a metal or plastic disc that sits between the machine's motor shaft and the cleaning pad or brush. It has a center connection that fits the machine, and a face that grips the pad.

When the motor turns the shaft, the pad driver spins the pad against the floor. A worn or wrong pad driver means the pad slips, the machine vibrates, or cleaning quality drops.

---

The Main Pad Driver Types

NP-9200 Pad Driver

The most common type. Two lugs on the driver match the two slots on the brush or pad. Fits Tennant, Viper, Nilfisk, Comac, and most Chinese OEM machines.

3-Lug Pad Driver

Three lugs with a center screw. More secure, used on Advance and older Nilfisk machines.

4-Lug Pad Driver

Used on European brands such as Karcher and Hako. Some use magnets, others use mechanical clips.

Center Lock Pad Driver

A simple center bolt design used on single-disc floor machines. The pad is held by a center retaining screw.

---

Sizes

Pad drivers come in sizes matched to your machine and pad:

13 inch for small single-disc machines
16 inch for standard walk-behind scrubbers
17 to 20 inch for mid-size machines
24 to 28 inch for ride-on scrubbers

The driver size must match both your machine's shaft and the pad you use.

Signs Your Pad Driver Needs Replacing

The pad slips or stops spinning during operation.
Bent or broken lugs from dropping the machine or hitting curbs.
A stripped center hole from overtightening.
Visible cracks in the disc body.

A pad driver should last several years with normal use, but heavy daily scrubbing can shorten its life.
