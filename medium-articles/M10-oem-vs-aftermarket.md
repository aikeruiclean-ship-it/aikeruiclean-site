# OEM vs Aftermarket Floor Scrubber Parts: Which Should You Choose?

**By Mark Xu | Sales Director at Aikerui Cleaning Technology**

If you own a floor scrubber, eventually you'll need replacement parts — brushes, squeegee blades, pad drivers, or batteries. And you'll face the classic question: **OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) or aftermarket?**

Here's the honest breakdown, from a manufacturer's perspective.

---

## The Short Answer

| Factor | OEM Parts | Aftermarket (Quality) |
|--------|-----------|----------------------|
| Price | $$$ (dealer markup) | **$$ (30-50% less)** |
| Quality | Good (spec-driven) | **Good to excellent** |
| Availability | Dealer dependent | **Direct from factory** |
| Warranty | 1 year | 1 year |
| Fit | Perfect (made for that brand) | **Compatible (same specs)** |

**Bottom line:** High-quality aftermarket parts from a real manufacturer offer **the same quality at 30-50% less**. The catch is finding a *real* manufacturer — not a middleman.

---

## What "OEM" Actually Means

OEM parts are made by the factory that produced the original machine — or licensed by them. For brands like Tennant, Nilfisk, or Karcher, "OEM" parts often come from their manufacturing partners in Asia.

**The reality:** Many "OEM" parts for major brands are manufactured in the same Chinese factories that produce aftermarket parts. The only difference is the brand name and the 2-3x price markup.

---

## The Aftermarket Trap: Trading Companies

Not all aftermarket parts are equal. The risk isn't the factory — it's the **middleman**:

| Type | What happens | Risk |
|------|-------------|------|
| Real manufacturer | Makes parts in their own factory, tested, consistent | Low |
| Trading company | Buys from various factories, no QC control | **High** — quality varies batch to batch |

**How to verify a real manufacturer:**
1. Ask for a **live video tour** of the factory floor
2. Request **certifications** (ISO 9001, CE)
3. Check if they have **360+ SKUs** in stock (a real manufacturer stocks breadth)
4. Ask about **materials** — "virgin nylon?" (real manufacturers use virgin, not recycled)

---

## When OEM Makes Sense

OEM is worth paying for when:

- **Under warranty** — using aftermarket parts may void machine warranty
- **Critical safety components** — brake parts, electrical (but these rarely need replacing)
- **You need absolute brand consistency** — corporate policy

---

## When Aftermarket Makes Sense

Aftermarket (from a real manufacturer) is the smart choice when:

- **Warranty period is over** (most machines: 1 year)
- **It's a consumable** — brushes, squeegee blades, pads (replaced regularly)
- **You want to cut costs** — save 30-50% on every replacement
- **You need parts fast** — direct from factory, 24h shipping
