Shampoo Disc Brush for Floor Scrubbers: Custom Nylon & Abrasive Brushes

By Mark Xu | Sales Director at Aikerui Cleaning Technology

A shampoo disc brush is a floor scrubber brush built for deep cleaning and shampoo application. It mounts on the brush deck, spins at 1000 to 2800 RPM, and applies cleaning solution evenly while scrubbing dirt from the floor. It is the standard choice for wash and wax programs on tiled, concrete, and sealed floors.

This brush is available in nylon and abrasive versions, both custom-made for your machine and your floor.

---

What a Shampoo Disc Brush Does

- Deep cleaning for daily and periodic maintenance
- Even shampoo and wax solution application across the floor
- Fits most walk-behind and ride-on floor scrubbers
- Custom sizes, materials, and colors available

The soft nylon brush plate holds liquid evenly, so cleaning solution works where it should. The abrasive version adds strong grinding and cutting power for stubborn dirt and grease.

---

Nylon vs Abrasive Shampoo Brushes

| Material | Best For | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nylon | Daily deep cleaning, even liquid application | Soft and thorough, holds solution evenly |
| Abrasive | Stubborn dirt, grease, industrial floors | Strong scrubbing and cutting power |

Compatibility

This shampoo disc brush uses the standard NP-9200 2-lug mounting, compatible with most floor scrubber brands.

- NP-9200 2-lug: Tennant, Nilfisk, Viper, and most Chinese OEM machines
- Motor speed: 1000-2800 RPM
- Machine type: walk-behind and ride-on floor scrubbers
