## Why the Brush Matters

The brush is the part of the floor scrubber that actually does the cleaning. It scrubs dirt off the floor so the squeegee can pick up the water. A worn or wrong brush means:

Streaks and film left behind. The brush missed the dirt, so the squeegee cannot pick it up.

Dirt pushed around instead of removed. Flattened or wrong bristles lose scrubbing power.

More chemical usage. Operators compensate for a bad brush by using more cleaning solution.

Floor damage. A wrong or worn brush can scratch concrete or epoxy.

Getting the brush right has an outsized impact on cleaning quality and cost.

---

## Nylon Brushes: Daily Floor Maintenance

Nylon brushes are flexible and wear-resistant, making them the default choice for daily floor maintenance.

| Feature | Why It Works |
|---------|--------------|
| Flexible bristles | Conform to floor contours, clean evenly |
| Wear-resistant | Long service life, keeps shape |
| Gentle on surfaces | Safe on sealed concrete, tile, epoxy |
| Low shedding | Fewer bristles lost, less mess |

Best for: workshops, malls, supermarkets, offices, and any facility that cleans daily.

---

## Steel Wire Brushes: Heavy-Duty Scouring

Steel wire brushes powerfully scour heavy oil and stubborn caked-on dirt that nylon cannot remove.

| Feature | Why It Works |
|---------|--------------|
| Powerful scouring | Removes heavy oil and caked-on dirt |
| Aggressive bristles | Deep-cleans rough concrete |
| Dense and firm | High scrubbing power per pass |
| Long service life | Handles harsh industrial use |

Best for: underground parking lots, factories, machine shops, and heavy industrial floors.
