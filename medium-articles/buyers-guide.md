# Don't Buy a Floor Scrubber Before Reading This — 5 Decisions That Make or Break Your Investment

Buying the wrong floor scrubber costs more than buying no scrubber at all. I've seen warehouses stuck with machines that can't fit through their aisles. Factories with scrubbers that can't handle their floor type. Operations that burned through $8,000 in unexpected parts and battery costs in the first 18 months because nobody calculated TCO.

Here are the five decisions that determine whether your scrubber pays for itself or becomes an expensive garage ornament.

---

## Decision 1: Walk-Behind or Ride-On?

This is the most consequential choice. Get it wrong and you either overspend by $10,000+ or buy something that can't keep up.

**Walk-behind**: $3,000-12,000. Compact. Maneuverable. 15,000-25,000 sq ft/hr. Best for facilities under 20,000 sq ft with narrow aisles or multiple floors.

**Ride-on**: $9,000-35,000. 2-3x faster. 25,000-80,000+ sq ft/hr. For any facility over 30,000 sq ft, a ride-on pays for itself through labor savings within 12-18 months.

If your facility is between 20,000-30,000 sq ft, lean toward ride-on — you'll grow into it.

---

## Decision 2: Disc Brush or Roller Brush?

Disc brushes spin flat — best for smooth, sealed floors like epoxy-coated concrete or tile. Roller brushes spin vertically and dig into grout lines and textured surfaces — mandatory for unsealed factory floors.

**Disc brush**: $45-75 to replace. Smooth floors.
**Roller brush**: $80-200 to replace. Textured or uneven floors.

Wrong brush = poor cleaning results + premature brush wear. Match the brush type to your floor, not the machine spec sheet.

---

## Decision 3: Lead-Acid or Lithium Battery?

Lead-acid costs $400-800 upfront. Lithium costs $1,200-2,400. But the real math:

| | Lead-Acid | Lithium |
|---|---|---|
| Charge time | 8-10 hours | 2-3 hours |
| Maintenance | Monthly watering + terminal cleaning | Zero |
| Replacement | Every 1-3 years ($500-800) | 5+ years ($0) |
| 5-Year Cost | $400 + $800 (maintenance labor) + $800 (replacement) = $2,000 | $1,800 (one-time) |

For single-shift: lead-acid works. For multi-shift: lithium pays for itself by year 3.

## Decision 4: The 5-Year TCO — Not the Sticker Price

| Machine | Purchase | 5-Year Parts | Battery | 5-Year Total |
|---------|----------|-------------|---------|-------------|
| Entry walk-behind | $4,500 | $2,500 | $800 | $7,800 |
| Mid walk-behind | $6,500 | $3,000 | $1,200 | $10,700 |
| Compact ride-on | $12,000 | $4,000 | $1,500 | $17,500 |
| Factory-direct ride-on | $9,000 | $2,500 | Included | $11,500 |

That $9,000 factory-direct ride-on costs $6,000 less over 5 years than the $12,000 dealer compact ride-on. TCO matters more than purchase price.

---

## Decision 5: Factory-Direct or Local Dealer?

| | Local Dealer | Factory-Direct |
|---|---|---|
| Machine Price | +30-50% markup | Manufacturer cost + margin |
| Parts Price | +40-100% markup | Wholesale |
| Service | Local, fast | Self-managed or 3rd party |
| Warranty | 2-3 years | 1 year (extendable) |
| 5-Year Savings | — | $7,000-32,000 |

If you have in-house maintenance capability or a 3rd party service provider, factory-direct saves massively. If you absolutely need same-day on-site service contracts, dealer is the safer bet.

## The ROI Math

Let's run real numbers. A 50,000 sq ft warehouse currently employs two janitors at $18/hr × 6 hrs/day:

- Current labor cost: $56,160/year
- Switch to one ride-on operator (2.5x faster): $22,464/year
- Machine cost: $9,000 purchase + $500/yr parts + $0 (lithium)
- Annual savings: ($56,160 - $22,464) - ($9,000/5 + $500) = $30,496/year
- **Payback: 4-5 months**

Your facility will almost certainly have different numbers. But the pattern is consistent: the machine pays for itself quickly, and factory-direct pricing accelerates the payback.

## 10-Point Buyer's Checklist

Before ordering from any manufacturer:

☐ Live video factory tour via WhatsApp
☐ Verify business registration on government database
☐ Check ISO/CE/SGS certification numbers
☐ Speak to 3 customer references in your country
☐ Order a sample unit first
☐ Confirm payment terms — L/C or Trade Assurance
☐ Calculate shipping + customs duty + inland delivery
☐ Verify spare parts availability and pricing
☐ Compare warranty terms in writing
☐ Calculate total landed cost — not just machine price
