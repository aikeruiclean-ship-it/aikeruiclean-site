# Aikerui — Industrial Floor Cleaning Machines Manufacturer

**Anhui Aikerui Environmental Protection Technology CO.,LTD** is a factory-direct manufacturer of industrial floor scrubbers, sweepers, and 360+ OEM replacement parts. Based in Hefei, Anhui, China. Since 2008.

## Products
- **Floor Scrubbers** — Walk-behind & ride-on (17-50 inch)
- **Floor Sweepers** — Industrial sweepers for warehouses & factories
- **Replacement Parts** — Disc brushes, squeegee blades, pad holders, roller brushes, clutch plates (compatible with Tennant, Nilfisk, Kärcher, Hako, Viper, Gaomei, Dulevo)
